## ⭐ 3 — Statement of the Paradox

### **Paradox A — Structural Dependency**
Validation gates must be embedded into structure.  
Therefore, **structure must exist first**.

### **Paradox B — Behavioral Dependency**
Behavior must be validated before it is embedded into structure.  
Therefore, **validation must exist first**.

These two statements appear mutually exclusive.

NDH resolves them through spiral construction.
