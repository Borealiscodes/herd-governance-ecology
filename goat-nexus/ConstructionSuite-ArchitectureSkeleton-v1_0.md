### **5.1 Structural Scaffolding Layer**

Defines the neutral, non‑activating frame:

- altitude‑neutral supports  
- non‑hierarchical logic beams  
- membrane‑pure boundaries  
- drift‑bounded geometry shells  

### **5.2 Validation Gate Embedding Points**

These are **slots**, not gates themselves:

- altitude_integrity_slot  
- drift_invariant_slot  
- membrane_purity_slot  
- unionized_logic_slot  
- adjacency_stability_slot  

These will be filled by the Validation Architecture.

### **5.3 Metadata Routing Channels**

- semantic flow
